# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Employees browse and open intern projects without remembering IP addresses or ports. Interns view their own projects. Administrators create intern accounts and projects, inspect port usage, and archive finished projects.

## Product Purpose

PortD (Algérie Télécom Internship Project Hub) is a small internal registry for projects, owners, assigned ports, directories, startup entry points, and internal URLs.

## Brand Personality

Clear, dependable, operational.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-page treatment, noisy infrastructure dashboards, opaque workflows, and decorative data visualisation that does not help users act.

## Design Principles

- Make the project URL and owner immediately findable.
- Present operations as simple records, tables, and actions.
- Keep port allocation visible without pretending to be a full monitoring platform.
- Use predictable navigation and familiar controls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use clear labels, keyboard-accessible controls, high-contrast text, visible focus states, and reduced motion support.
